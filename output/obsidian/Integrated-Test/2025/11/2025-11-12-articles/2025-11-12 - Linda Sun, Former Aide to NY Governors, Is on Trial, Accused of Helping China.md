## 📰 본문

When F.B.I.
agents raided a $3.5 million home of Linda Sun, a former aide to two New York governors, on Long Island’s North Shore in July 2024, they came away with a Rolex, a diamond ring and a Ferrari Roma.

The luxury items, prosecutors say, were the product of payoffs from the Chinese Communist Party, whose agenda Ms. Sun was covertly carrying out in Albany as an adviser and aide to governors Andrew M.

Cuomo and Kathy Hochul.
Now, Ms. Sun and her husband, Chris Hu, are on trial in Federal District Court in Brooklyn.
She faces charges that she had worked as an unregistered agent of the Chinese government, and he is accused of money laundering, among other charges.

Opening arguments in the trial are expected Wednesday. The case is the latest in which federal prosecutors claim that the Chinese government is seeking to influence American politics.
