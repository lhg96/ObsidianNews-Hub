## 📰 본문

Footage shows the moment Russia's first anthropomorphic robot, AIdol, fell just seconds after its debut at a technology event in Moscow.

The robot was being led on stage to the soundtrack from the film 'Rocky', before it suddenly lost its balance and fell.

Assistants could then be seen scrambling to cover it with a cloth - which ended up tangling in the process.
